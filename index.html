FIELD GUIDE

FLIGHT CONTROLS

Tap the left or right half of the screen to change lanes. On a keyboard, use ← / → or A / D. Tap the pause icon or press P to pause. Titans can launch a rocket with the button beside the lower-left thumb area; on a keyboard press F.

MISSION OBJECTIVES

Avoid incoming red hazards. Collect crystals to score, grow your combo, and bank gems after each run. Collected power-ups activate automatically. Complete daily challenges and achievements, then claim their rewards from the Rewards page.

POWER-UP SYSTEMS

⬡ShieldAbsorbs one collision (two with Shield 2.0).
⌁MagnetPulls nearby gems toward your fighter.
◷Slow timeSlows hazards for a short time.
ϟLaserAutomatically fires at the next hazard ahead. Twin Laser targets two hazards.
➤Titan rocketsManually destroy the nearest block ahead. Rockets are limited per flight; Guided Rack adds extra ammunition.

FIGHTER ABILITIES

Dart is balanced. Ghost unlocks at combo x10 (27 consecutive gems) and has a smaller hitbox, faster lane changes, and a 280–1,200 speed range. Titan unlocks at combo x15 (42 consecutive gems), starts shielded, and runs from 100–750 speed with manual rockets. Market fighters have their own passive abilities.

HANGAR & REWARDS

Use My Hangar to review your fighters, skins, technologies, and power-up upgrades. A glowing Rewards button means one or more rewards are ready to claim.

RUN MODES

Classic is an endless survival run. Sprint challenges you to survive 60 seconds. Zen removes hazards so you can fly and collect in peace.
